Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you order.

Are your links white-hat?
Yes. We place content on real websites with real traffic. No PBNs, no link farms and no automated spam. Every site is checked for traffic, indexing and spam signals before it joins our list.
How fast will my links go live?
Most guest posts and niche edits are published within 3 to 10 working days. Digital PR depends on the publication. You receive the live URL as soon as each placement is published.
Do you write the content?
Yes. Our editors write original, SEO-optimised articles for every placement. You can also send your own article, and you can approve content before it is published.
Can agencies resell your services?
Yes. Our white-label service delivers unbranded reports, so you can present the work to your clients under your own brand.
What metrics do you show?
We report DR (Ahrefs), DA (Moz), organic traffic, country and language for every site, plus the live URL after publishing.
How do I pay?
We accept PayPal, Payoneer, Wise and card payments. For larger orders, part-payment can be arranged.
What if a link is removed?
If a placement is removed within the guarantee period agreed for your order, we replace it at no cost.
