FAQ

Questions clients ask us

What does MayoTechX do?
We are a full-service technology company: SEO, link building and digital PR; Google, Meta and TikTok ads; web, WordPress and e-commerce development; custom software, mobile apps and AI automation.
Do you work with international clients?
Yes. We are remote-first and work with clients across North America, Europe, the Middle East and Asia, scheduling calls in your time zone.
How do you price projects?
Link building is priced per placement, SEO and ads are monthly retainers, and websites, apps and software are quoted per project with milestones. Dedicated teams are billed monthly.
Who owns the work?
You do. Websites, code, designs, ad accounts and content belong to you.
Are your links white-hat?
Yes. Real websites with real traffic — no PBNs, link farms or automated spam.
How fast can you start?
Most projects kick off within a few days of approval. Ads can go live within days; link placements typically publish in 3–10 working days.
